Road to 50

VO2max 50 and beep test level 10.0. No rush, just keep ticking.

Training in the heat
  • Run early morning or evening. Do hard sessions in the coolest conditions you can find, or indoors.
  • Run easy days by effort (you can hold a conversation), not heart rate. Expect HR 5-15 bpm higher than usual.
  • Garmin reads VO2max low in the heat. Watch the trend over weeks, not one number.
  • Allow 10-14 days to adapt. Drink before and after, and cut a session short if you feel unwell.
